Approach

My Philosophy

I believe recruiting works best when it is both disciplined and human — clear processes, reliable data, and technology that handles the administrative load so your team can focus on building relationships and finding people who will genuinely thrive.

Focusing on What Matters

Speed without quality is just noise. I focus on Quality-of-Hire because filling a seat is not the goal — building a team is. The best hiring processes create visibility long after the offer is signed, tracking how people perform, how they fit, and whether the investment paid off.

Data as a Conversation Starter

Metrics are most powerful when they drive candid conversations with business leaders — not when they sit in a dashboard. I use time-to-fill, offer acceptance rates, and source effectiveness to tell a story that moves decisions forward.

Trends & Insights

On My Radar

📊

Moving Past "Time-to-Fill"

Speed is a vanity metric if retention is low. I track how top organizations shift core KPIs toward long-term Quality-of-Hire, measuring business impact 12 to 18 months into the role.

🤖

Agentic AI in the Recruiting Stack

AI should remove administrative friction, not the human element. I'm focused on how workflow automation handles scheduling and data-mapping, freeing teams to build real relationships.

📈

The Evolution of the Fractional Executive

High-growth companies are leveraging flexible, fractional HR leadership to build complex infrastructure before scaling full-time teams. I'm closely tracking this shift.

My Framework

Iterative TA Maturity Model

Most recruiting functions get stuck in reactive mode. This is the framework I use to move organizations from demand-driven hiring to a sustainable, intelligence-led sourcing engine — in three deliberate stages.

01

Stage One

Build

Sourcing

Demand-driven; managed reactively by team lead

Process

Basic screen-and-submit workflow; demand requests come from hiring leaders

Technology

Core ATS configured; foundational reporting in place

AI Tools

Initial exploration for job description writing and basic candidate matching

Vendor Use

High; external agencies fill sourcing gaps

02

Stage Two

Optimize

Sourcing

Agile model; dedicated point of contact per hiring leader

Process

Streamlined experience with active feedback loops

Technology

ATS fully utilized; real-time pipeline dashboards live for hiring managers

AI Tools

AI used for outreach personalization, scheduling, and sourcing workflow automation

Vendor Use

Reduced; internal sourcing handles the majority of requisitions

03

Stage Three

Scale

Sourcing

Proactive, intelligence-led; passive talent pipeline always active

Process

Firmwide consistency; continuous improvement cycles built in

Technology

ATS integrated across the full talent lifecycle; automation reduces manual work firmwide

AI Tools

AI embedded across sourcing, screening, and reporting; team focuses on relationships and judgment

Vendor Use

Minimal; reserved for niche or surge scenarios only
